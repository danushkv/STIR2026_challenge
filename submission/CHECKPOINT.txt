The consistency check (repair_disparity, CONSIST_K=8 ABS=8.0 MAD=3.0) is the
geometric-consistency step from Peng's 2025 write-up: disparity is a difference
of two independently tracked points, so their errors add and one bad
triangulation is otherwise emitted as a confident 3D point. Replacing grossly
inconsistent disparities with the neighbourhood median improves ALL FIVE
thresholds (tag agg_e44_match_fix4):

Paired clip bootstrap: +0.0167, 95% CI [-0.0011, +0.0473], P(better)=0.912 --
